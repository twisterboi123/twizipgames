FAQ

Questions

Does it work for TikTok, streaming and Zoom?

Yep. It doesn't touch your camera or any other app, it just lights up your screens. So it works with TikTok, OBS, Zoom, Teams, Discord, whatever you use.

Can it turn on by itself when I join a call?

Yes. Turn on "Turn the glow on when my camera turns on" in Settings. When your webcam starts, the light comes on, and it goes off when the camera stops. It never sees or records what your camera sees.

Is it actually free?

Yep. No trial, no account, no ads, and it doesn't collect your data.

It's way too bright.

Turn it down. Brightness goes from 5% to 100%, and Ctrl + Alt + ↑ / ↓ works anytime. Warm white at around 40% is nice for late-night calls.

My whole screen is lit up. How do I get out?

Press Ctrl + Alt + L, press Esc, or double-click the light to open the app.

How do I show it on my Discord?

Open the app, go to Settings and turn on the Discord option. Discord has to be open on your PC.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Monitor Glow → Uninstall. Your settings get removed too.
